# Soybean growth stages

## Vegetative stages (VE to Vn)
Soybean development is described with the Fehr and Caviness system. VE is
emergence (the seedling breaks the soil surface); VC is when the two
unifoliolate leaves have unrolled. From then on each new fully developed
trifoliolate leaf on the main stem adds a stage: V1 (first trifoliolate), V2,
V3 and so on. A new node typically appears every 3 to 5 days early on. During
vegetative growth the plant builds the leaf area and root nodules that later
support pod fill, so early stand losses and weed competition matter most here.

## Reproductive stages (R1 to R8)
R1 beginning bloom: one open flower at any node. R2 full bloom: an open flower
at one of the two uppermost nodes. R3 beginning pod: a pod 5 mm long at one of
the four uppermost nodes. R4 full pod: a 2 cm pod at one of the four uppermost
nodes. R5 beginning seed: a seed 3 mm long in a pod at one of the four
uppermost nodes. R6 full seed: a green seed fills the pod cavity. R7 beginning
maturity: one normal pod has reached its mature colour. R8 full maturity: 95 %
of pods have their mature colour. Yield is most sensitive to stress (drought,
heat, defoliation, disease) from R3 through R6, the pod and seed filling
period, because flowers and young pods abort under stress.

## Canopy closure and what it means for drone imagery
Canopy closure is when leaves from neighbouring rows meet and the ground
between rows is no longer visible from above. In 15-inch (38 cm) rows this
usually happens around V5 to R1, roughly five to seven weeks after emergence
depending on planting date and variety; 30-inch (76 cm) rows close later,
often around R2 to R3, and sometimes not completely. Before closure an RGB
drone image separates crop rows from the soil between them, so gaps, thin
stands and vegetation between rows can be measured. After closure the whole
field reads as vegetation: cover indices saturate, small differences in
vigour are harder to see, and vegetation between rows is the crop itself, not
a weed signal. Multispectral indices such as NDRE keep more sensitivity in a
closed canopy than RGB greenness does.
